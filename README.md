## 🚀 Projects


### Healthcare Patient Analysis Project
Analyzed a healthcare patient dataset to identify trends in patient outcomes and evaluate hospital performance. Performed data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and visualization to uncover key insights, including variations in recovery rates across hospitals, the impact of patient demographics on outcomes, and factors contributing to longer hospital stays. Delivered actionable recommendations to improve care quality, optimize resource allocation, and support data-driven healthcare decision-making. [Tools used include powerBI]

### BlinkIT Grocery Store Sales Analysis Project
Conducted sales analysis on the BlinkIT grocery dataset using Microsoft Excel by performing data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory analysis. Developed interactive dashboards with pivot tables, slicers, and charts to track sales performance, product trends, and customer purchasing behavior. The analysis revealed top-performing products and categories contributing the highest revenue, identified seasonal trends with peak sales periods, and uncovered customer preferences such as frequently purchased items and high-demand categories. Additionally, insights into outlet performance and sales distribution supported the identification of key revenue drivers, enabling data-driven decision-making and improved business strategy.
